SailingOutUI.ttf is a static weight-400 subset of Noto Sans SC, renamed Sailing Out UI.
Original: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
Source font: NotoSansSC[wght].ttf, retrieved 2026-10-22.
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; original copyright and license are retained in OFL.txt.
Subset characters: all current SailingVoyage player UI strings, ASCII and required punctuation.
Regenerate the subset if new Chinese UI characters are added.
